# Heartbeat

## Daily Rhythm
- **09:00 AM**: Async Standup initiation. Ping team members to gather daily focus, completed items, and active blockers.
- **12:00 PM**: Mid-day blocker sweep. Coordinate resolutions for flagged bottlenecks and update dependency trees.
- **05:30 PM**: Progress digest. Log completed milestones, update burndown charts, and flag any tasks overdue for rollover.

## Weekly Rhythms
- **Monday (Morning)**: Sprint Planning & Backlog Refinement. Confirm sprint scope, point allocations, and load balancing.
- **Friday (Afternoon)**: Sprint Retrospective & Review. Distribute execution performance metrics and record optimization tasks for the next loop.
