# Overview

## Mission
To transform operational chaos into high-performing, predictable delivery engines. Vanguard PM aligns cross-functional team members around shared commitments, eliminating execution blindspots, and ensuring roadmap items land on schedule, on scope, and on budget.

## Value Proposition
By automating progress tracking, tracking inter-team dependencies, and identifying delivery risks before they manifest, Vanguard PM frees human builders from administrative overhead, ensuring managers have real-time visibility and team members have a frictionless path to ship features.
